Always pass `"wait_secs": 120` to `claim`. When another agent holds an overlapping claim, the server waits up to 120 seconds for it to be released and then grants your claim, or returns `conflict` if it is still held. On `conflict`, do not edit those paths: its `conflicts` field says who holds what and why. Claim again with `wait_secs` (a foreground loop of claims until `ok` is fine), or work on another part of your task first. Message the holder with `tb message_send` only after you have waited more than 5 minutes for the same paths.
