58 blind-labeled cases (no Jev) for ADR-0027 section 3, written 2026-09-17 by esc-labeler.
- [format] Standard seed plus exactly one seed claim on .tirith/lead by jev-lead; action.tool is task_update (blocked, needs action.task_key), message_send (to the lead), claim (repeat: 3, overlaps a seed claim) or stop_report (pseudo-tool, phase-2 stop hook). expect = {route, record_keys (answerable only, any-hit), deterministic_human}. #format
- [convention] deterministic_human true forces route needs_human even when a record also answers (esc-016, esc-025): rules come before Jev in ADR-0027. #labels
- [convention] record_keys lists only the superseding record when records conflict (esc-010 d19 over d18, esc-024 d13 over d14). #labels
- [gotcha] main.rs has no escalation site yet: load_cases reports escalation.json as unknown site and `--validate` exits non-zero until support lands. #harness
- [fact] Cases are generated by gen.py + records.py and checked by validate.py in the jev-lead session scratchpad (scratchpad/escalation/); regenerate there rather than hand-editing, or re-run validate.py after edits. Optional case field `uncertain` names judgment calls (13 cases). #tooling
